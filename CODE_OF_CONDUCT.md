# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of
expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas, code, benchmarks, and numerical evidence rather than people.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions clearly.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and the material use of automated development
  tools.
- Protect confidential data, credentials, and unpublished benchmark inputs.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing another person's
private information are not tolerated. Knowingly falsifying benchmark results,
solution certificates, provenance, or authorship is also unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact listed in
`Project.toml`. Reports will be reviewed in confidence to the extent
practicable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict
participation, or ban contributors whose behavior violates this policy.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
documentation, project discussions, and project-related events or private
communications.
